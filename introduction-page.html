📖 HABITISM — User Guide
Everything you need to build consistency, earn coins, and watch your companion bloom.

🌸 Welcome

HABITISM helps you build life-changing consistency through daily habits, streak tracking, rewards, and real accountability. Complete your daily tasks before midnight, earn coins, protect your streak, and watch your living companion bloom.

1. Creating Your Account

  • Go to the Sign Up page.
  • Fill in:
    • Name (max 80 characters, letters and spaces only)
    • Username (max 16 characters, letters and numbers only, unique)
    • Email (max 254 characters, unique)
    • Password (8–64 characters: at least 1 letter, 1 number, 1 special character)
    • Who do you love the most? (max 80 characters, letters and spaces only)
🔒 Important: Core Verifier
The question "Who do you love the most?" is your account recovery key. Write it down and do not share it with anyone. If you forget your password or your account is compromised, this answer is required to recover your account.

2. Logging In

  • Go to the Login page.
  • Select WHO ARE YOU?:
    • 👤 Standard User (default)
    • 👑 Administrator
  • Enter your Username or Email and Password, then click Log In.
  • If you select the wrong role for your account, you will see a friendly error asking you to select the correct role.

3. Accepting Your Commitment

Before using the dashboard, you must accept the daily commitment on the Rules page:

  • Check both pledge boxes.
  • Click I Accept The Commitment • Enter Dashboard.
  • This locks in your promise to complete daily habits before 11:59 PM.

4. Daily Habit Tracking

  • Dashboard: You will see your Level and a list of daily habits. Check the box next to each task you complete. Complete at least 5 tasks to keep your streak alive.
  • Task Modes:
    • Developer Levels: Ready-made habits (Level 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
    • Custom: Create your own 5 daily habits.
  • Switch between modes using the ⚙️ Mode button in the navbar.

5. Calendar & Streak Glow

Your calendar date glows based on how many tasks you complete today:

Tasks Completed Glow Color Meaning
5 🟢 Green Met daily goal
6–7 🟠 Orange Exceeded daily goal
8+ 🔵 Light Blue Outstanding
  • The glow reflects your committed list for today.
  • You can browse other levels without losing your glow.
  • The glow and checkboxes reset at midnight.

6. Coins & Piggybank

  • Earning Coins:
    • Check a task → +10 coins to your Piggybank.
    • Uncheck a task → −10 coins from Piggybank (prevents double-counting if you check and uncheck repeatedly).
  • Claiming Coins:
    • Coins earned during the day stay in your Piggybank (🐷 icon in navbar).
    • At the end of the day, click Claim to Main Balance to move them to your main coin counter (🪙 icon).
    • Main balance coins are used to buy Streak Freezes.

7. Streak Freezes

A Streak Freeze (❄️) protects your streak from resetting if you miss a day.

  • Cost: 50 coins in the Shop.
  • Automatically consumed if you miss a commitment (no Facebook post is published).
  • You start with 1 free Streak Freeze.

8. Living Companion

Your companion evolves as your streak grows:

  • 0–1 days: Nurturing Seed 🌰
  • 2–6 days: Growing stages 🌱 → 🌿
  • 7+ days: Radiant Bloom 🌸 → 🪷

Click the companion widget in the navbar to view your current stage.

9. Weekly Certificate

Complete a full 7-day streak to unlock a printable Weekly Certificate.

  • Click 🏆 Certificate in the navbar.
  • If unlocked, click Print / Save Certificate to save or print your certificate.

10. Feedback

Have an idea, concern, or bug report?

  • Click 💬 Feedback in the navbar.
  • Select type: Idea, Concern, or Bug Report.
  • Write your message (max 1,000 characters) and click Submit Feedback.

11. Forgot Password & Account Recovery

If you forget your password:

  1. Click Forgot Password? on the login page.
  2. Enter your Username/Email, Account Creation Date, Last Remembered Password, and answer your Core Verifier: "Who is the person you love the most?"
  3. Enter your desired new password (min 8 chars) and submit the request.
🎫 Recovery Ticket
• You will receive a unique ticket number (format: AAAAAA000000 — 6 uppercase letters + 6 numbers).
• Save this ticket number. It is important for tracking your progress.
• Your request will be reviewed within up to 72 hours.
• A post will be made to the Habitism Facebook Page with your ticket number and status (Approved or Rejected).
• If approved, your password is updated to your new password.
• If you already have a pending recovery request, you cannot submit another until it is resolved.

12. Troubleshooting

Problem Solution
Login failed Check your username/email and password. Make sure you selected the correct role (Standard User or Administrator).
Calendar not glowing Complete at least 5 tasks on your committed list for today.
Coins not in main balance Claim them from your Piggybank first.
Forgot password not working Make sure your core verifier answer matches what you entered at signup, and check that you don't already have a pending ticket.
Facebook post not published Depends on Facebook access token configuration by the platform.
