Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 4, 2026

You can ask HABITISM to delete your account and all data connected to it at any time. This applies whether you signed up with an email and password or with Facebook Login.

Step 1: Remove HABITISM from your Facebook account (Facebook users)

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations).
  3. Find HABITISM and click Remove.

This stops HABITISM from accessing your Facebook information. It does not by itself delete the data we already hold, so please also do Step 2.

Step 2: Ask us to delete your HABITISM data

Send an email to drahcirordonez@gmail.com with the subject HABITISM Data Deletion Request and include:

  • The name or username on your HABITISM account
  • The email address you signed up with (or your Facebook name if you used Facebook Login)
  • Optional: ask us to also remove any accountability post about you on the Habitism Facebook Page

What we delete

  • Your account profile (name, username, email, Facebook ID and Facebook name)
  • Your habits, checks, commitments, streaks, calendar history, coins and piggybank balance
  • Any accountability post records about you in our database, and, on request, the matching public posts on our Facebook Page

How long it takes

We confirm your request by email and complete deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

For more on how we handle data, read our Privacy Policy.
