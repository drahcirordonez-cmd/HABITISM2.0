# 🌸 HABITISM — User Guide

## Welcome to Habitism
**HABITISM** helps you build life-changing consistency through daily habits, streak tracking, rewards, and real social accountability. Complete your daily tasks before midnight, earn coins, protect your streak, and watch your living botanical companion bloom!

---

## 1. Creating Your Account
1. Visit the **Sign Up** page (`signup.html`).
2. Fill in your registration details:
   - **Full Name**: Up to **80 characters** (letters and spaces only).
   - **Username**: Up to **16 characters** (letters and numbers only, unique).
   - **Email Address**: Up to **254 characters** (valid email format, unique).
   - **Password**: **8 to 64 characters** (must include at least 1 letter, 1 number, and 1 special character).
   - **Who do you love the most? (Core Verifier)**: Up to **80 characters** (letters and spaces only).

> 🔒 **Important: Your Core Verifier**  
> The question *"Who do you love the most?"* is a critical piece of information used when recovering your account — whether you forgot your password or your account is compromised. Please take note of your answer and **do not share it with anyone**.

---

## 2. Logging In
1. Go to the **Log In** page (`login.html`).
2. Under **"WHO ARE YOU?"**, select your role:
   - **👤 Standard User** *(default)*
   - **👑 Administrator**
3. Enter your **Username or Email** and **Password**.
4. Click **Log In as User**.
   - *Tip: If you accidentally select the wrong role toggle for your account, Habitism will show a friendly reminder asking you to select the matching role.*

---

## 3. Accepting Your Daily Commitment
Before entering your dashboard for the first time, you must accept the accountability pledge on the **Rules & Commitment** page (`rules.html`):
1. Read the daily rules carefully.
2. Check **both pledge boxes** confirming you understand how daily streaks and Facebook social accountability work.
3. Click **I Accept The Commitment • Enter Dashboard**.
   - This locks in your promise to complete your committed daily habits before **11:59 PM**.

---

## 4. Daily Habit Tracking

### Your Dashboard
- On the right card, you will see your active **Habit Level** (or Custom Routine) and your daily checklist.
- Check the box next to each habit as you complete it throughout the day.
- Complete **at least 5 tasks** on your committed list every day to keep your streak alive!

### Task Modes (Developer Levels vs. Custom Routine)
Click the **⚙️ Mode** button in the top navigation bar to switch between two ways to challenge yourself:
1. **⚡ Developer Levels**: Progress through curated, science-backed habit levels (Level 1: *Taking the first steps*, Level 2: *Let's keep it up*, Level 3: *Keep moving forward*, Level 4: *Meditation*, Level 5: *Prime your environment*).
2. **🎯 Custom Habits**: Create and customize your own tailored list of 5 daily habits.

### Committing to a List
- Click **Commit to this?** on the checklist footer to lock your daily calendar glow to your chosen habit list for today.
- Once committed, you can freely browse other levels without losing your progress or calendar glow.

## 5. Calendar & Streak Glow
Your calendar date on the left card illuminates dynamically based on how many tasks you complete on your committed list today:

| Tasks Completed | Glow Color | Tier Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **5 Tasks** | 🟢 **Green Glow** | **Met Daily Goal** — Your streak is safe for today! |
| **6–7 Tasks** | 🟠 **Orange Glow** | **Exceeded Daily Goal** — Great extra effort! |
| **8+ Tasks** | 🔵 **Light Blue Glow** | **Outstanding Discipline** — Maximum daily mastery! |

- Your calendar glow reflects your **committed list** for today.
- Checkboxes reset automatically at **midnight (12:00 AM)** so you start fresh each morning.

## 6. Coins & Piggybank System

### Earning Coins (🐷 Piggybank)
- **Check a task**: **+10 Coins** are added to your daily **Piggybank**.
- **Uncheck a task**: **−10 Coins** are removed from your Piggybank.
- This smart buffer prevents accidental double-counting if you check and uncheck tasks while organizing your day.

### Claiming Coins to Your Main Balance (🪙)
- Coins earned during the day stay inside your **Piggybank** (`🐷` in the navbar and checklist footer).
- When you finish your tasks for the day, click **Claim to Main Balance** to transfer your Piggybank coins into your permanent **Main Coin Balance** (`🪙`).
- *Note: Claiming your Piggybank locks your checked habits for that list for the rest of the day.*

## 7. Streak Freezes (❄️)
A **Streak Freeze** acts as an emergency shield that protects your streak from resetting to `0` if you miss a day.
- **Starting Bonus**: Every new account starts with **1 free Streak Freeze**.
- **Shop Cost**: Purchase additional Streak Freezes in the **Reward Shop** (click the `🪙` or `❄️` pill in the navbar) for **50 Coins** each.
- **Automatic Protection**: If you miss your daily commitment before midnight and have a Streak Freeze available, it is automatically consumed to save your streak, and no penalty post is published to Facebook.

## 11. Forgot Password & Account Recovery
If you forget your password or need to recover your account:
1. Click **🔑 Forgot Password?** on the Login page.
2. Complete the **Account Recovery Form**:
   - **Username or Email Address** associated with your account.
   - **Account Creation Date** (the date you registered).
   - **Last Remembered Password**.
   - **Who is the person you love the most?** (your Core Verifier answer from sign-up).
   - **Desired New Password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Submit Request for Review**.

### 🎫 Your Recovery Ticket
- Upon submission, you will receive a unique **Recovery Ticket Number** in the format **`AAAAAA000000`** (6 uppercase letters followed by 6 digits, e.g., `KXMZRT482910`).
- **Save this ticket number!** It is essential for tracking your recovery status while keeping your identity private.
- Please allow up to **72 hours** for your request to be reviewed.
- Once a decision is made, an update is published on the **Habitism Facebook Page** displaying your **Ticket Number** and status (**Approved** or **Rejected**).
- If approved, you can immediately log in using your new password!
- *Note: You can only have one pending recovery request at a time.*

## 12. Troubleshooting & FAQ

| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Login failed** | Double-check your username/email and password, and make sure you selected the matching role under **"WHO ARE YOU?"**. |
| **Calendar date is not glowing** | Make sure you clicked **"Commit to this?"** on your active habit list and completed at least **5 tasks** today. |
| **Earned coins aren't in my main balance** | Click **"Claim to Main Balance"** inside the Piggybank box on your dashboard to transfer your daily earnings. |
| **Cannot uncheck a habit** | Once you claim your Piggybank coins for a list today, those completed checkmarks are locked for the day. |
| **Cannot submit another password recovery** | You already have a pending request under review. Check the Habitism Facebook Page with your recovery ticket number. |
