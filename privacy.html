Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

HABITISM ("we", "our", "the app") is a gamified habit-tracking web app available at https://habitism2-0.onrender.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, email address and password when you sign up.
  • Facebook Login (optional): if you choose "Continue with Facebook", we receive your Facebook ID, public profile name and, if you allow it, your email address. We do not receive your Facebook password, friends list or private messages.
  • App activity: the habits you create, which habits you check off each day, your commitments, streaks, calendar history, coins and piggybank balance.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and save your progress across devices.
  • To calculate streaks, rewards and your calendar.
  • To operate the social accountability feature described below.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

3. Social accountability posts (please read)

HABITISM has an accountability feature. If you commit to a daily list and do not complete it by the deadline (11:59 PM by default), the app may publish a public post on our Facebook Page, Habitism, saying that you made a commitment but did not complete it. The post may include your name or Facebook profile name and the habits on your committed list. You agree to this when you press "Commit". If you do not want this, do not use the commit feature. You can also use a Streak Freeze, where available, to be shielded from a post.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with: (a) Meta/Facebook, when you use Facebook Login or when a post is published to our Facebook Page; and (b) our hosting provider (Render), which stores the app's data on our behalf.

5. Data storage and retention

Your data is stored on our hosting provider's servers and in your browser's local storage. We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see our Data Deletion page), we delete your account data. Posts already published publicly on our Facebook Page are removed on request.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time.
  • You can remove HABITISM from your Facebook account any time under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
  • Follow the steps on our Data Deletion page to delete your account and data.

7. Children

HABITISM is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
